## Abstract

The Adaptive Campus Canteen Pre-Ordering and Smart Pickup System is a web-based application designed to improve the efficiency of food ordering and pickup in college canteens. During break hours, students often experience long queues, overcrowding, and delays in receiving their meals, leading to inconvenience and loss of valuable time.

The proposed system enables students to browse the available menu, place food orders in advance, select a preferred pickup time, and receive a digital token or QR code for quick food collection. This approach simplifies the ordering process, minimizes waiting time, and provides a more convenient dining experience for students.

A key feature of the proposed system is its adaptive demand management mechanism, which monitors incoming orders and responds to changing demand patterns. When the number of orders for a particular food item exceeds a predefined threshold, the system is designed to notify canteen staff to prepare additional food in advance. It also aims to update food availability and pickup schedules based on order conditions, helping staff manage resources more effectively.

The system is planned to provide separate modules for students, canteen staff, and administrators. Students will be able to register, log in, browse the menu, place and track orders, and collect food using a digital token or QR code. Canteen staff will be able to manage menu items, monitor incoming orders, update order status, and respond to adaptive alerts. Administrators will be able to manage users, monitor transactions, generate reports, and analyze order trends.


## Objectives

* Enable students to pre-order food online.
* Allow students to select preferred pickup times.
* Provide digital tokens or QR codes for quick food collection.
* Reduce waiting time and overcrowding in the canteen.
* Monitor incoming orders and changing demand patterns.
* Notify canteen staff when demand for a food item becomes high.
* Improve food preparation and resource management.
* Reduce food shortages, unnecessary delays, and food wastage.
